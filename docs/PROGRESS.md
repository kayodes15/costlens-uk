# CostLens UK release notes

## Version 1.0.1

- Added CostLens icons for browser tabs and home-screen bookmarks.
- Kept the header visible while scrolling and moved keyboard focus to the page heading at the top after navigation.

## Version 1

- The economic snapshot starts with UK figures and supports selecting a council. It shows house prices, private rents, earnings, inflation and interest rates, with coverage and source dates. Scotland and Northern Ireland rent estimates can use a wider rental market area. Northern Ireland district earnings come from NISRA.
- Salary and pay-rise calculators show changes in purchasing power and pay targets. The relocation calculator compares disposable income and the time needed to recover moving costs.
- Home ownership tools cover mortgage payments and purchase costs, buying now versus waiting, and renting versus buying. Supported residential purchase taxes use dated rules and accept a manual override.
- Charts include accessible tables. Calculators support browser print reports and process financial inputs on the device.
- The [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md), [worked examples](TEST-CASES.md) and [data source register](DATA-SOURCES.md) explain how to reproduce the results and interpret the published figures.

See the [release checklist](RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md) for release criteria.
