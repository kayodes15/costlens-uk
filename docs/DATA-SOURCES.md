## M5 geographical joins

Checked 26 November 2026 UK. The [ONS local housing tool](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/housingpriceslocal) supplies council names and codes, the displayed Scottish BRMA, and the best match and published significant overlaps for Northern Ireland. The [Belfast example](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/housingpriceslocal/N09000003/) describes the postcode-overlap method and lists Belfast, South East Northern Ireland and Lough Neagh Upper. ONS assigns the NI chart identifiers used to join the corresponding workbook names. Only the identifiers and geographic matches are extracted from the local pages. Rent estimates come from the PIPR workbook. Source credit is Office for National Statistics, Housing prices in your area, Crown copyright under the [ONS terms](https://www.ons.gov.uk/help/terms-conditions). M5 covers council selection. Town aliases require verified mapping and terms before use.
