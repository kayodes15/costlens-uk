# Data licences

The MIT licence covers the project's software. External datasets and official guidance retain their respective licences.

The [source register](docs/DATA-SOURCES.md) lists the applicable licence, attribution and limitations for each source. For material covered by the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), retain the source attribution and licence link in the dataset manifest and accompanying documentation. Observe any publisher-specific notice and third-party exclusions. Keep sources with unverified reuse out of distributed data until resolved, using manual inputs where applicable.
